Glossary

Term Meaning
Aggressor / taker Incoming order that trades immediately against resting liquidity
Ask / offer Sell side; resting sell limits
BBO Best bid and offer (best bid + best ask)
Bid Buy side; resting buy limits
CLOB Continuous limit order book
Crossed book Best bid ≥ best ask — invalid post-state for our engine
Depth Liquidity available at successive price levels
FIFO First-in first-out within a price level (time priority)
Fill Execution / trade against an order
FOK Fill-or-kill: full qty now or reject, no partials
GTC / DAY Rest until cancel / until session end
Iceberg Shows partial size; refreshes display qty
IOC Immediate-or-cancel: match now, cancel remainder
L1 / L2 / L3 Top of book / aggregated depth / full order-by-order book
Level All resting orders at one price
Limit order Cap (floor) on buy (sell) price; may rest
Liquidity Resting size available to trade against
Locked market Best bid == best ask
Lot Quantity quantum
Maker Resting party to a trade
Market order Take liquidity now; no rest price
Marketable Limit that can trade immediately given BBO
Matching engine Component that applies priority rules and emits trades
Midprice Average of best bid and best ask
NBBO National best bid/offer (US equities consolidated)
Order book Data structure of resting orders
Partial fill Trade for less than order’s remaining qty
Post-only Accept only if order rests (maker); reject if it would take
Price priority Better prices match before worse
Price-time priority Price first, then arrival time
Pro-rata Same-price allocation by size share (not our v1)
Resting Live on the book awaiting fill/cancel
Spread Best ask − best bid
STP Self-trade prevention
Sweep / walk the book Aggressor consumes multiple price levels
Tick Minimum price increment; we store integer ticks
TIF Time-in-force
Time priority Earlier resting orders at a price trade first
Trade / execution Matched qty at a price between maker and taker
